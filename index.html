The two day transformation

In and out in two days

Most garage upgrades mean long projects, mess and weeks of waiting. Our polyaspartic installations are completed over two days, with proper preparation and curing so your floor is built to last rather than rushed.

01

Day one: prep, basecoat and flake

Every successful installation starts with preparation. Our team grinds the concrete to open the pores and remove dirt, oils and old sealers, so the coating bonds directly to the concrete itself and not to surface debris. Once the surface is ready we roll on a durable basecoat and broadcast decorative flakes across the entire floor for a seamless, fully covered finish.

02

Day two: scrape and topcoat

After curing overnight we return to scrape and smooth the flakes, reducing sharp edges and touching up any spots. The final step is a clear polyaspartic topcoat that locks everything in. That top layer is UV stable, chemical resistant and built to handle everything Colorado weather sends at it.

Questions

Frequently asked

How long does installation take?

Most NuWave Garages installations are completed over two days. Our streamlined process minimizes disruption while ensuring proper preparation, curing and long term durability, so your new floor is built to last rather than rushed.

Are the coatings slip resistant?

Yes, our coatings include textured finishes designed to enhance grip and prevent slips, making your space safer for both residential and commercial use, even in wet or high traffic conditions.

Can I choose colors or patterns?

Absolutely. We offer a wide range of color options and custom design patterns to match your preferences, allowing you to personalize your floor with a look that suits your home or business.

Do you serve commercial clients?

Yes, we provide expert flooring solutions for commercial properties, including warehouses, showrooms and retail spaces. Our team ensures each project meets the performance, safety and aesthetic needs of your business.

What makes polyaspartic coatings better?

Polyaspartic coatings cure quickly, resist UV damage and provide superior durability compared to traditional epoxy. They are ideal for Colorado's climate and deliver long lasting results with excellent protection and minimal maintenance.
